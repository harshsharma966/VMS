Frequently Asked Questions

Find answers to common questions about Smart-Cam VMS and our services.

General Questions

What is Smart-Cam VMS?

Smart-Cam VMS is an advanced video management system specifically designed for construction sites. It provides real-time monitoring, automatic timelapse generation, cloud storage, and powerful analytics to help you manage your construction projects more effectively.

How does the free trial work?

We offer a 14-day free trial with full access to all features. No credit card is required to start. During the trial, you can test all our features, connect your cameras, and experience the full power of Smart-Cam VMS. After the trial, you can choose a plan that fits your needs.

What cameras are compatible with Smart-Cam?

Smart-Cam supports most IP cameras with RTSP/RTMP streams. We're compatible with major brands including Hikvision, Dahua, Axis, and more. If you're unsure about your camera's compatibility, contact our support team for assistance.

Can I access my cameras from mobile devices?

Yes! Our web application is fully responsive and works on smartphones and tablets. You can view live streams, playback recordings, and manage your cameras from any device with a web browser.

Technical Questions

How secure is my video data?

We take security seriously. All video streams are encrypted end-to-end using industry-standard AES-256 encryption. We're SOC 2 certified and GDPR compliant. Your data is stored in secure data centers with multiple redundancy layers and regular security audits.

What's the uptime guarantee?

We offer a 99.9% uptime SLA for all plans. Our infrastructure is built on enterprise-grade servers with automatic failover and redundancy. In the rare event of downtime, we'll credit your account proportionally.

How does cloud storage work?

Your video footage is automatically uploaded to our secure cloud servers. Storage is allocated based on your plan (100GB to 10PB+). Older footage is automatically archived but remains accessible. You can download recordings anytime, and we keep multiple backup copies for redundancy.

What internet speed do I need?

For optimal performance, we recommend: Upload speed of 5-10 Mbps per HD camera, Download speed of 10-20 Mbps for viewing live streams. Our system automatically adapts to your bandwidth, so it will work with slower connections at reduced quality.

Billing & Account

Can I upgrade or downgrade my plan?

Yes! You can change your plan at any time from your account dashboard. Upgrades take effect immediately. Downgrades will be applied at the end of your current billing cycle. We'll prorate any charges accordingly.

What payment methods do you accept?

We accept all major credit cards (Visa, MasterCard, American Express, Discover), PayPal, and bank transfers for annual plans. Enterprise customers can also pay via invoice with NET 30 terms.

Is there a setup fee?

No, we don't charge any setup fees for Starter and Professional plans. Enterprise plans may include optional white-glove onboarding and training services at an additional cost.

What's your refund policy?

We offer a 30-day money-back guarantee on all annual plans. If you're not satisfied within the first 30 days, we'll issue a full refund. Monthly plans are non-refundable, but you can cancel anytime with no penalties.

Can I cancel my subscription?

Yes, you can cancel your subscription at any time from your account settings. There are no cancellation fees. Your access will continue until the end of your current billing period, and we'll export all your data for you.

Support

What support channels are available?

We offer email support for all plans, priority email support for Professional plans, and 24/7 phone support for Enterprise customers. We also have comprehensive documentation, video tutorials, and a community forum.

How long does it take to set up?

Most customers are up and running within 30 minutes. The process is: 1) Sign up for an account, 2) Add your camera details (IP, credentials), 3) Start streaming! We provide step-by-step guides and can assist with setup if needed.

Do you offer training?

Yes! We offer free webinar training sessions for all customers. Enterprise customers receive personalized on-site or virtual training sessions as part of their onboarding process.

Still Have Questions?

Our support team is here to help. Contact us and we'll get back to you within 24 hours.
